PROMPT 3.1 — FLOATING-POINT BOUNDARY FIX
========================================

Prompt Summary:
Correct the floating-point precision issue affecting the educational remaining-time safeguard. Use math.isclose() with an appropriate absolute tolerance to prevent numerical representation differences from bypassing the safeguard.

Problem Identified:
Subtracting 23.9 from 24 hours may produce a floating-point value slightly greater than 0.1.

Action Taken:
Implemented the is_at_or_below_classroom_time_threshold() helper function using a direct comparison and math.isclose(), with a relative tolerance of 0.0 and an absolute tolerance of 1e-12.

Outcome:
The calculation logic checks the numerical safeguard before calculating the hourly estimate.

Verification:
Test 08 confirmed that 23.99 hours triggered the safeguard and withheld the hourly estimate. Exact boundary verification at 23.9 hours remains a final testing check.
